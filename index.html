Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Glass Recon™ Application & Performance

Does Glass Recon work on tinted windows? expand_more
Yes! Glass Recon™ is strictly an exterior treatment. Since window tint film is applied to the interior of your glass, our formula is 100% safe to use on any vehicle with tinted windows. It cleans and coats the outside surface without ever touching your tint.
Will this remove hard water spots and acid rain? expand_more
Absolutely. Glass Recon uses mild abrasives and molecular cleaners designed specifically to strip away calcium deposits (hard water spots), acid rain etching, road tar, and stubborn tree sap that regular glass cleaners leave behind.
How long does the hydrophobic coating last? expand_more
One application typically provides 3 to 6 months of protection, depending on your local weather conditions and wiper usage. When you notice water stops beading off as aggressively, simply re-apply a small amount to top up the shield.
Is it safe for my wiper blades? expand_more
Yes. In fact, Glass Recon helps your wipers last longer. By removing the rough, sticky layer of oil film and contaminants from the glass, your wiper blades glide smoothly over a frictionless surface, reducing chatter, squeaking, and wear.
Can I use this on side mirrors and rear windows? expand_more
Yes! We highly recommend it. Side mirrors and rear windows don't have wipers, so having a hydrophobic coating is even more critical for visibility during storms. Glass Recon ensures these surfaces stay clear without you having to wipe them.
