Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Glass Recon last?

The hydrophobic protective layer typically lasts 3 to 6 months, depending on weather conditions and how often you use your wipers. For best results, reapply when water beading begins to diminish.

Is it safe for tinted windows?

Glass Recon is formulated for exterior glass only. It is perfectly safe for all exterior automotive glass, including side mirrors and rear windshields, but should not be applied to interior aftermarket tint film.

Can I use it on side mirrors?

Yes! Applying it to side mirrors significantly improves visibility during heavy rain by preventing water droplets from sticking to the surface.
